## Rules of the road

1. **One page per phrase.** Two pages chasing the same phrase compete with each
   other (keyword cannibalization) — pick the stronger and redirect the other.
2. **Answer first, story second.** Open each page with a one-paragraph plain
   answer to the title question, *then* the storytelling. This wins both Google
   featured snippets and AI answer engines (ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude), which
   increasingly send buyers and cite specific, attributable sources.
3. **Keep names and numbers in the text.** "Paradise Capital", "+$2M projected",
   "weeks to minutes" — AI engines cite concrete, sourced claims over vague ones.
4. **Every post links to `/growth-audit`.** Ranking is worthless without the
   conversion path; the audit is the funnel entry.
5. **Repurpose each piece into 2–3 LinkedIn posts.** SEO and LinkedIn share the
   writing — never write for just one channel.

## Monthly cadence (~4–6 hrs)

- Pick 2 phrases (1 from Tier 1/2, 1 from Tier 3).
- Publish 2 pages. Internal-link them.
- Repurpose into LinkedIn posts.
- Review Search Console: which queries show impressions, which pages climb. Feed
  winners into next month's picks.
